OUTREACH ACTIVITIES

We organize a range of community outreach activities focused on heritage, which involve engaging local communities through workshops, exhibitions, festivals, and educational programs. These initiatives aim to raise cultural awareness, preserve traditions and ensure the continuity of cultural practices for future generations.
