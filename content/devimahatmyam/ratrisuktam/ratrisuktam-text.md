### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 001॥

### Sanskrit

ॐ रात्री व्यख्यदायती पुरुत्रा देव्यक्षभिः।<br>
विश्वा अधिश्रियोधित॥१॥

### Transliteration

oṁ rātrī vyakhyadāyatī purutrā devyakṣabhiḥ|<br>
viśvā adhiśriyodhita ||1||

### English Translation

> The goddess Night that comes, saw with eyes of vision in many planes. She holds high all the splendours.

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 002॥

### Sanskrit

ओर्वपा अमर्त्या निवतो देव्युद्धतः।<br>
ज्योतिषा वाधते तमः॥२॥

### Transliteration

orvapā amartyā nivato devyuddhataḥ|<br>
jyotiṣā vādhate tamaḥ||2||

### English Translation

> The goddess immortal, she fills the vast, the high and the low places. She repels darkness with light.

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 003॥

### Sanskrit

निरु स्वसारमस्कृतोपसं देव्तायती।<br>
अपेदुहासते नमः॥३॥

### Transliteration

niru svasāramaskṛtopasaṁ devtāyatī|<br>
apeduhāsate namaḥ||3||

### English Translation

> The goddess that comes, prepared her sister Uṣas.
> And the darkness leaves.

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 004॥

### Sanskrit

सा नो अद्य यस्यावयं नि ते यामन्नविक्ष्महि।<br>
वृक्षे न वसति वयः॥४॥

### Transliteration

sā no adya yasyāvayaṁ ni te yāmannavikṣmahi|<br>
vṛkṣe na vasati vayaḥ||4||

### English Translation

> She is ours now. May we find refuge in her movements as a bird its dwelling on the tree.

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 005॥

### Sanskrit

नि ग्रामासो अविक्षत नि पद्वन्तो नि पक्षिणः।<br>
नि श्येनासश्चिदर्थिनः॥५॥

### Transliteration

ni grāmāso avikṣata ni padvanto ni pakṣiṇaḥ|<br>
ni śyenāsaścidarthinaḥ||5||

### English Translation

> The hosts of men had refuge in her. The footed, the feathered and the hawks as well seek her intensely.

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 006॥

### Sanskrit

यावया वृक्यं वृकं यवयस्तेनमूर्यं।<br>
अथा न सुतरा भव॥६॥

### Transliteration

yāvayā vṛkyaṁ vṛkaṁ yavayastenamūryaṁ|<br>
athā na sutarā bhava||6||

### English Translation

> Assail the she-wolf. Separate from us the he - wolf, and the thief, Then in the billowings ferry us with ease

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 007॥

### Sanskrit

उप मा पेपिशत्तमः कृष्णं व्यक्तमस्थित।<br>
उप ऋणेव यातय॥७॥

### Transliteration

upa mā pepiśattamaḥ kṛṣṇaṁ vyaktamasthita|<br>
upa ṛṇeva yātaya||7||

### English Translation

> She has come near to me giving clear shapes to the black darkness. O Dawn, absolve me as from debts.

#### \*\*

### ॥ Rātri Sūktam ॥ Śloka 008॥

### Sanskrit

उप ते गा इवाकरं वृणीष्व दुहितार्दिवः।<br>
रात्रि स्तोमं न जिग्युषे॥८॥

### Transliteration

upa te gā ivākaraṁ vṛṇīṣva duhitārdivaḥ|<br>
rātri stomaṁ na jigyuṣe||8||

### English Translation

> I have offered to thee like kine. Accept O Night, daughter of Heaven, the laud as for a conqueror.
