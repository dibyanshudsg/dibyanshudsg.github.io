### ॥ प्रथमोऽध्यायः॥ ॥ prathamo'dhyāyaḥ ॥ The First Episode

#### ---------

### ॥ अथ श्रीदुर्गासप्तशती॥ ॥ atha śrīdurgāsaptaśatī ॥

#### \*\*

### Sanskrit

विनियोगः<br>
अस्य श्री प्रथमचरित्रस्य। ब्रह्मा ऋषिः।महाकाली देवता। गायत्री छन्दः। नन्दा शक्तिः।<br>
रक्तदन्तिका बीजम्। अग्निस्तत्त्वम्।ऋग्वेदः स्वरूपम्। श्रीमहाकालीप्रीत्यर्थे<br>
प्रथमचरित्रजपे विनियोगः।

### Transliteration

viniyogaḥ<br>
asya śrī prathamacaritrasya | brahmā ṛṣiḥ | mahākālī devatā | gāyatrī chandaḥ | nandā śaktiḥ|<br>
raktadantikā bījam | agnistattvam | ṛgvedaḥ svarūpam | śrīmahākālīprītyarthe<br>
prathamacaritrajape viniyogaḥ |<br>

### Sanskrit

। ध्यानम्।<br>
ॐ खड्गं चक्रगदेषुचापपरिघाञ्छूलं भुशुण्डीं शिरः<br>
शङ्खं सन्दधतीं करैस्त्रिनयनां सर्वाङ्गभूषावृताम्।<br>
नीलाश्मद्युतिमास्यपाददशकां सेवे महाकालिकां<br>
यामस्तौत्स्वपिते हरौ कमलजो हन्तुं मधुं कौटभम्॥<br>

### Transliteration

| dhyānam |<br>
om khaḍgaṁ cakragadeṣucāpaparighāñchūlaṁ bhuśuṇḍīṁ śiraḥ<br>
śaṅkhaṁ sandadhatīṁ karaistrinayanāṁ sarvāṅgabhūṣāvṛtām |<br>
nīlāśmadyutimāsyapādadaśakāṁ seve mahākālikāṁ<br>
yāmastautsvapite harau kamalajo hantuṁ madhuṁ kauṭabham ||<br>

### English Translation

Meditation on Mahākālī Wielding in her hands the sword, discus, mace, arrow, bow, iron club, trident, sling, human head and conch, she has three eyes and ornaments decked on all her limbs. She shines like a blue stone and has ten faces and ten feet. That Mahākālī I worship whom the lotus-born Brahma lauded in order to slay Madhu and Kaiṭabha when Hari was asleep.
